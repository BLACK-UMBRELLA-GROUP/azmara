# Original AZMARA building catalogue — first visual set

Date: 10 October 2026. Authority: DEC-048. Mode: built-in ImageGen; no CLI/API key.
Use case: stylized-concept. These are four transparent contact-sheet assets, not
31 production-ready individual level-progressing sprites or implemented mechanics.
Thirty new designs plus the existing Town Hall cover 31 supplied reference roles.

## Originality and scope

The supplied Ikariam screenshots establish building purposes only. Their pixels,
prose, red-roof visual identity, resource costs, timing, percentages and unlock
values were not imported. Generation used only our original Town Hall v3 as a
palette/perspective reference. Fictional architecture is inspired, not a claim
of historical reconstruction. Prayer Area replaces Temple as explicitly directed;
its possible satisfaction effect remains unbalanced. Chronos’ Forge is secular
work planning/timekeeping/fabrication, with no deity, miracle or divine power.

The sheets use four columns and two rows, row-major. Civic and industry have
eight drawings; trade and war have seven, with a blank bottom-right cell.
CSS selects cells without modifying or slicing the original raster. Ground is
local to each design. Do not place all buildings automatically in the town:
the 23 plot roles and starting Hall + 22 empty sites remain unchanged.
