## What changed

- Three original raster images: quiet founding ground, maintained mature
  ground, and shaded buff stone material. The new rear rock band removes the
  distant photographic horizon inside this pilot, without rebuilding the wall.
- Ground edges feather into same-density mirrored soil sampling for camera
  margins. An initial hard rectangular edge was rejected after capture.
- At 50, three live-anchor-derived quarter floors join building foundations.
  Short civic/trade street accents replace the outlining perimeter web. A first
  ring-like routing attempt was rejected after visual inspection; long detours
  were removed rather than forced across buildings.
- Tight low-opacity contact shading/aprons integrate with existing sprite
  bases. The prior general CSS radial bed is suppressed only in this pilot,
  avoiding a second heavy blanket shadow.
- Empty sites use soft cleared earth and wooden stakes, not grass diamonds,
  plus buttons or blue rectangles. The wall's dedicated reserve remains the
  original marker, consistent with the wall-work pause. Original labels,
  keyboard focus and selectable controls remain with the caller.
- Existing clipped palms, gardens, planters and two closed fountain crops are
  arranged in safe pockets. No rills, rivers, extra owned buildings, actors or
  ambient movement were added. Props are omitted when full-body clearance is
  not available; paving is cosmetic floor, not collision/navigation.

## Scope and limits

The exact gate is desktop presentation + Staggered + integer completed/review
Hall level 0, 1 or 50. It is not a release/debug permission gate. Dev/Gameplay
can both display the eligible art; modes still share the same save. Every
intermediate level, other layout and compact presentation uses the previous
renderer. Abrupt endpoint transitions are deliberate pilot limits, not final
level progression.

The same 23 IDs, actual live projected anchors, five layouts, fixed roles,
resources, saved levels and construction receipt/deadline rules remain. No
wall source/assets, native files, hosted staging or preserved Step 5 evidence
were changed. Existing building pixels and progression schedules survive.
The original illustration renderer remains: this is **2.5D**, not a 3D engine.

It is still a static ground-first draft. Entrances use conservative crop-ground
anchors rather than authored sockets, and not every building gets a separate
road spur. Broader planted shade masses, human-scale street activity, realistic
entrance connections, per-band ground art, phone exports and all five layouts
are NOT complete. The wall still has its previously recorded extra-tower,
gate-target and phone-framing problems; none is claimed fixed or obsolete.

Existing PNG scales are not an underlying calibrated 3D camera. Ground can
lose detail at the maximum close zoom; a passed transform check is not final
maximum-zoom art approval. The shown ground direction is accepted under
DEC-073; broader finished-art/coverage acceptance is not implied.
