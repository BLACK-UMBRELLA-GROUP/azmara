## DEC-022 — Town worker sliders and citizen income

- Date: 7 October 2026.
- Source: Founder explained choosing workers with sliders, limited by the
  town's free population and the production site's worker maximum, with
  remaining citizens providing hourly income. Exact rates are deferred.
- Approved direction: each town assigns its own population separately to
  timber and specialist production. A citizen cannot work in both assignments
  simultaneously; total assigned workers cannot exceed the available population.
- Approved limit: each assignment is constrained by both the applicable site
  maximum and the population not assigned elsewhere. A site with room for
  100 workers cannot receive more than 100 from a town with only 100 available.
- Approved economic relationship: unassigned citizens provide hourly town
  income. Allocating them to resource work reduces that income-producing group.
- Example interpretation: the stated 250 timber workers, 250 specialist workers
  and 500 unassigned citizens sum to 1,000 people, not the stated 100. This is an
  illustrative arithmetic interpretation, not a selected starting population
  or balancing value.
- Unresolved: whether a shared site's maximum applies independently per town
  or across the island, worker limits by level, production rates, currency,
  income per citizen, upkeep, other roles and allocation handling if population
  falls. This does not approve research staffing or military population rules.
- Boundary: rule documentation under `DEC-015` only. No worker controls,
  production, income simulation or prototype changes are authorised here.
