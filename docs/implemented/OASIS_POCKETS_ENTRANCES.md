all noncandidate RGBA and alpha>8. Complete connected bodies/shadows down to

## Scoped integration finding

Besides `prototype/oasis-town.js` and two module-cache strings in `index.html`,
`app.js` needed a small presentation-metadata correction: its `appearanceFor`
now passes the source art actually displayed by artwork review. Previously it
sent saved levels while DOM sprites showed review stages, risking a level50
socket on a level1 picture or the reverse. The saved/actual level still drives
local material quality. Owner was told before this scoped extension; the
pre-change app copy and supplementary baseline receipt were retained. The
guard verifies every byte outside `appearanceFor` unchanged, including save,
queue and plot logic. No engine, library, CSS, camera or native change.
